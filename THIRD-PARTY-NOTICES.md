# Asset and dependency notices

My MIT license covers the original code and documentation in this repository. It doesn't cover Nuclear Option content or other third-party material.

For Eyeball-XL, I adapted the ARAD-116's geometry and flight settings and the Eyeball's optical behavior. For the AGK-4 Lance, I adapted the AGR-24 Kingpin's prefabs, flight settings and pod mesh, and textured the new rocket from the Kingpin's texture atlas. The authored meshes and prefabs contain geometry and serialized values copied from those game assets. Materials, audio and other game objects are referenced at runtime, not shipped. That content belongs to its owners, and you need a purchased copy of Nuclear Option to use or build this mod. The source package excludes extracted game assemblies, texture dumps and placeholder assets.

The Lance pod's AGK-4 stencil is original art that covers the stock "AGR-24" marking. No modified game texture ships with the mod.

The Blueprinter Unity template is copyright 2026 Nikkorap and is provided under its MIT license in `unity/LICENSE`. Blueprinter Editor is pinned as a Unity package dependency. BepInEx, the Blueprinter runtime and Unity packages are separate dependencies and aren't included in the manual-install package.

I used AI for all the custom art because I don't have the skills to make the models and artwork myself. GPT-6 Astra made the Eyeball-XL's art and Claude Opus 5.5 made the Lance's. The reused game assets were made by their original creators. I'm open to discussing contributions of human-made models and art.
